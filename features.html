Features — Built for campus life

UniVerse combines communication, learning, collaboration and campus services into one modern experience. Below you will find a detailed description of every core feature and how it helps students, faculty and campus staff.

Core Experience

Each feature is designed to be discoverable, secure and integrated with campus processes. Below you will find a professional explanation, common use cases and recommended best practices for administrators and students.

👤

Create Account

Create an account using email or institutional sign-in. Accounts store profile, department, level and enrollment metadata so UniVerse can provide tailored content and secure access to university-only resources.

✅

Email Verification

Verification ensures an account's email is owned by the student. UniVerse supports standard verification flows and secure action handling compatible with Firebase Auth action codes.

🧾

Profile Setup

Profiles include photo, department, level, courses and optional student ID verification. Profiles are used for matching, recommendations and display in directory searches.

💬

Private Chat

End-to-end style private messaging model with rich media, attachments and read receipts. Designed with moderation tools, reporting and block/ignore controls for user safety.

📚

Faculty, Department & Level Matching

Smart matching connects students to peers and groups by department, faculty and year level. Matching reduces friction when forming study groups, sharing handouts and coordinating tutorials.

📝

Course Discussion

Structured channels for each course where students and staff can post notes, ask questions, upload handouts and pin important material. Integrated with timetable and tutorials for context.

🎓

Department Captains & Approval

Verified department captains are nominated representatives who can approve content, pin notices and validate resources. Captain approval helps reduce misinformation and centralize official campus communication.

📢

Announcements & Notices

Staff and captains can publish department notices, timetables, exam information and official announcements that appear prominently to affected users.

🧾

Resources & Quick Links

Centralized links for student portals (JAMB, NELFUND), handouts, past questions, forms and downloadable materials with access controls and version history.

🏆

Premium Verified Badge

Verified accounts (staff, captains, official pages) receive a premium badge to increase trust. Verification follows a documented process and is revocable by moderation teams.

🧮

GPA / CGPA Calculators

Integrated calculators let students compute GPA/CGPA across sessions and simulate grade scenarios. Exportable results can be saved to profiles or shared within groups.

🔔

Realtime Notifications

Push notifications for urgent department notices, private messages and assignment deadlines. Built with Firebase Cloud Messaging for cross-platform delivery.

Feature details and integration guides

Each feature uses Firebase (Auth, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging) with security rules that limit access by university, department and role. Best practice: enforce server-side rules and use short-lived tokens for privileged operations (uploads, captain approvals).

Common use cases

  • Study group coordination — Students create level-specific groups, schedule tutorials and share handouts.
  • Official notices — Department captains post verified notices and pin exam timetables.
  • Resource distribution — Lecturers upload handouts and past questions that are discoverable from course pages.
  • Lost & Found — Campus community posts missing item reports that are searchable and moderated.

Examples & screenshots

Admin & student best practices

  1. Admins: enforce department-level security rules and audit captain approvals regularly.
  2. Students: keep profiles up-to-date (department, level) to receive the most relevant content.
  3. Moderation: use reporting tools and temporary suspensions to keep the community safe.
  4. Backups: export agreed-upon public notices for archive and compliance.

Feature FAQs

How does Department Matching work?
Department Matching uses profile metadata (university, faculty, department, level) to recommend groups and channels. Users opt-in during onboarding and can change their department from profile settings.
Who can become a Department Captain?
Captains are nominated by existing captains or staff, verified via student ID or staff endorsement, and approved by the UniVerse moderation team following campus rules.
