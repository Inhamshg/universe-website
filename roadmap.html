Product Roadmap

Our roadmap outlines planned features, timelines and priorities. We collaborate with university partners to prioritize work that improves campus life.

Timeline

  1. Q3 2026 — AI Study Assistant (contextual notes, question answering, summarization), Scholarship listings pilot, improved resource search.
  2. Q4 2026 — Voice rooms & lightweight video calls for group study, Campus Marketplace beta.
  3. Q1 2027 — LMS integrations (synchronizing timetables, graded assignments), Clubs & Events module.
  4. Q2 2027 — Advanced privacy controls, parental consent flows (where required), and enterprise admin dashboard.

Planned features

AI Study Assistant

Assist students with focused study help: summarize lecture notes, generate quizzes, and explain concepts. Designed for privacy-first operation with optional on-device models where feasible.

Voice Rooms & Video Calls

Real-time audio rooms for study groups and lightweight video calls integrated with course channels and timetables.

Scholarships & Opportunities

Centralized scholarship listings, eligibility filters and application tracking for students.

Campus Marketplace

Peer-to-peer listings for textbooks, accommodation and services with trusted reviews and verified captains as moderators.

How we prioritize

We prioritize features that increase academic impact, improve safety, and reduce administrative overhead. Partners and captains inform priority decisions through pilots and feedback cycles.
