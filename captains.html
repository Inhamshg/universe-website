Department Captains

Department Captains represent their departments, help moderate groups and validate official notices.

Who is a Department Captain?

A Department Captain is a trusted student leader appointed to represent a department or faculty on UniVerse. Captains are visible as verified accounts and have moderation privileges within their department channels.

Requirements

  • Active enrolled student in the department they represent.
  • Good standing with the university (no active disciplinary sanctions).
  • Willingness to follow community guidelines and moderation policies.
  • Provide proof of enrollment (student ID or staff endorsement).

Responsibilities

  1. Approve official notices and announcements posted to the department channel.
  2. Moderate discussions, resolve minor disputes and escalate serious issues to UniVerse moderators.
  3. Coordinate with staff for timetable updates, tutorials and official events.
  4. Help onboard new students to department resources and channels.

Approval Process

The approval process balances verification and community trust:

  1. Nomination: an existing captain, staff member, or student organization nominates a candidate.
  2. Verification: the candidate submits proof of enrollment or staff endorsement.
  3. Review: UniVerse moderation reviews the submission and may request additional validation.
  4. Activation: upon approval, the account receives a verified captain badge and moderation privileges for their department channel.

Rules for Captains

  • Captains must not abuse moderation privileges or post misleading information.
  • Captains should respect privacy and avoid sharing personal data without consent.
  • Captains are expected to act as neutral facilitators for official academic communication.
  • Violation of rules may lead to badge revocation and loss of privileges.

Benefits

Captains receive recognition in the platform, improved tools for posting official notices, and support from UniVerse for moderation and training.
