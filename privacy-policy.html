Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026. This Privacy Policy describes how UniVerse collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use our website and mobile applications.

1. Summary

UniVerse (“we”, “our”, “us”) is an academic social network for university students. We collect and process information necessary to provide secure authentication, messaging, course collaboration, resources distribution and campus notices. Our platform uses Firebase services (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging) to deliver these features. This document explains what we collect, why, how we protect it, and your rights.

2. Data we collect

Account and identity data

Email address, display name, profile photo, university, faculty, department, level, student ID (if provided for verification), and any account metadata you supply during registration or profile setup.

Content you create

Messages (private and group), posts, comments, likes, uploaded files, attachments, images and any content you choose to submit.

Service & usage data

Usage metrics, device information (model, OS version), app version, IP address, timestamps for sign-ins and actions, and diagnostic logs necessary for maintenance and security.

Communications & notifications

Push notification tokens for Firebase Cloud Messaging and email communications history for support and operational messages.

Cookies & tracking

Cookies and similar technologies used on the website for authentication, preferences, analytics and security (see our Cookie Policy below).

3. How we use your data

  • Authentication and account security (Firebase Authentication).
  • Realtime messaging and collaboration (Firebase Realtime Database).
  • File uploads, media storage and content delivery (Firebase Cloud Storage).
  • Push notifications for urgent campus notices and messages (Cloud Messaging).
  • Personalization and matching (department, level) to recommend groups and channels.
  • Fraud prevention, abuse detection, and moderation.
  • Service analytics and performance monitoring.

4. Legal basis and lawful processing

Where applicable, we process personal data based on consent (for marketing), contractual necessity (providing core features), legitimate interests (security, product improvement) and legal obligations (recordkeeping for compliance). If you are subject to specific data protection laws, you may have additional rights explained below.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We share information only as necessary to provide the service:

  • With service providers (Google/Firebase) who host or process data on our behalf.
  • With your university or campus administrators when content is published to official channels (only with role-based controls and your profile consent where required).
  • When required by law, to comply with legal process or to protect rights and safety.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets — notice will be provided to affected users.

6. Data retention

We retain account and profile data for as long as your account exists. Content created by users (messages, posts) is retained according to the retention policies set by your university or the community settings; some content may be archived or deleted on request. We retain transactional logs and diagnostics for security and troubleshooting for a limited period (typically 90 days) unless longer retention is required by law.

7. Data deletion & account removal

You may request account deletion via the Delete Account page or by contacting help.universe.app@gmail.com. When you request deletion we will:

  1. Delete your profile from public listings and prevent further login.
  2. Remove or anonymize personal data from active databases (subject to technical limitations and backups).
  3. Retain certain records as required for legal compliance or to complete investigations (kept for a limited period and access-controlled).

Files in shared groups or messages may be retained if other users referenced them; in such cases we will anonymize or indicate the account has been deleted.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security practices: TLS for data in transit, access controls and Firebase security rules for data at rest. Sensitive operations (captain approvals, file access) require role-based checks and short-lived tokens. While we strive to protect data, no system is 100% secure; report suspected breaches to help.universe.app@gmail.com immediately.

9. Third-party services

The platform integrates with Google Firebase services. Please review Google’s privacy terms for Firebase. We may use third-party analytics and crash-reporting tools; these processors act under our instructions and do not have independent rights to use your data beyond providing services to us.

10. International transfers

Data processed through Firebase may be stored or processed outside of your country. We take steps to ensure adequate protections are in place when transferring data internationally, including contractual protections with providers.

11. Your rights

  • Access: request a copy of data we hold about you.
  • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your account and personal data subject to legal constraints.
  • Object or restrict processing: where applicable, you may object to certain processing activities.
  • Data portability: request a machine-readable export of your data.

To exercise these rights contact: help.universe.app@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

12. Children

UniVerse is intended for university students. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, contact us to request deletion.

13. Cookies

We use cookies for authentication, preferences and analytics. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Significant changes will be communicated through the app or via the email associated with your account. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates the most recent revision.
