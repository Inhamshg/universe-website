Delete your UniVerse account

We’re sorry to see you go. This page explains what is deleted, what may remain temporarily and how to submit a deletion request. This flow is designed to be compliant with Google Play and general data protection best practices.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the UniVerse app and go to Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  2. Confirm your identity by entering your password or following the email verification link.
  3. Confirm deletion. Your account will be scheduled for deletion immediately or after a short grace period to allow reversal.
  4. If you cannot access your account, email help.universe.app@gmail.com with your registered email and a deletion request.

What is deleted

When you delete your account we will:

  • Remove your profile and public-facing data (display name, photo).
  • Delete your private chats and messages from active databases where technically possible.
  • Remove personal identifiers from backups and logs in accordance with our retention policy.

What may remain temporarily

Copies of data may persist for a limited time in backups or logs used for debugging, fraud prevention and legal compliance. Shared content that other users have saved or forwarded may remain; in those cases we will anonymize personal identifiers where possible.

Google Play compliance notes

We follow Google Play policy by providing a clear in-app deletion flow and handling account removal without requiring account creation. We also provide data export tools on request in a machine-readable format before deletion.

Reversing a deletion

If your deletion is within the grace period (typically 14 days) you can contact support to restore your account. After the grace period deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

Contact

To request deletion manually or ask questions, email help.universe.app@gmail.com or call +2348139927759. For privacy inquiries see our Privacy Policy.
