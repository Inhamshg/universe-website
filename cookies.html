Cookie Policy

This policy explains how UniVerse uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and web app.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store information about your preferences and activity.

Types of cookies we use

Essential cookies

Required for authentication, session management and security. Without them the service will not function properly.

Preference cookies

Store user preferences like theme (dark/light) and language.

Analytics cookies

Collect anonymous usage information to improve performance and user experience (e.g., page views, device types).

Marketing cookies

Used only if you opt into marketing communications to measure campaign effectiveness. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings and opt-out of analytics or marketing cookies. Disabling essential cookies may affect core functionality.

Third-party cookies

We may allow third-party services (analytics providers, content delivery networks) to set cookies as described above. These third parties have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
