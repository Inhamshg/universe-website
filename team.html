Engineering & Product

Our developers and product managers work in small, cross-functional teams to ship features with a strong focus on reliability and student value.

  • Frontend: React Native & web UI specialists
  • Backend: Realtime systems, Firebase architects
  • Data & AI: analytics, privacy-first models and research
  • QA & Reliability: automated tests, monitoring and incident response

Community & Operations

Moderation, partnerships and community teams coordinate with universities and captains to ensure a healthy and useful experience for students.

  • Moderators — review reports, support appeals and enforce policy
  • Partnerships — onboard universities and student bodies
  • Support — help students with account and technical issues

Department Captains

Department Captains are elected or nominated students who act as liaisons for official notices and help moderate department channels. See the Captains page for details on responsibilities and becoming a captain.
