Community Guidelines

Our goal is a respectful, safe academic community. These guidelines explain expected behaviour for posts, comments and chats.

1. Be respectful

Treat others with courtesy. Avoid harassment, hate speech, threats, or personal attacks. Disagreements are allowed but keep them constructive.

2. No harassment or discrimination

Content that targets a person or group based on protected characteristics (race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, etc.) is prohibited.

3. Academic integrity

Do not use UniVerse to facilitate cheating. Sharing complete answers to active assessments, exam leaks, or facilitating academic dishonesty is prohibited.

4. No spam or solicitation

Unsolicited advertising, repeated posting or off-topic solicitations in course or department channels are not allowed. Contact support for approved campus announcements.

5. Fake accounts & impersonation

Do not create accounts impersonating others or fake institutional roles. Verified badges are reserved for authentic staff and captains following our verification process.

6. Illegal content

Do not post or distribute illegal material (copyrighted works without permission, illicit substances, or instructions for illegal activities).

7. Privacy & personal data

Do not share another user's private information (doxxing). Respect privacy and use reporting tools if personal data is shared without consent.

8. Moderation & enforcement

Reports will be reviewed by moderators. Consequences include warnings, content removal, temporary suspension, or account termination for severe or repeated violations.

9. How to report

Use the in-app reporting tools or contact help.universe.app@gmail.com with details (links, screenshots). For emergencies or threats to safety contact local authorities.

10. Appeals

If you believe moderation was incorrect, file an appeal to support with relevant context. Appeals are reviewed by a separate team where possible.

11. Best practices

  • Keep posts on-topic for the channel.
  • Use clear titles and summaries for shared resources.
  • Use department captains for official notices.
  • Report problems promptly and preserve evidence.
