Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about accounts, features, privacy, verification, captains and support.

General

1. What is UniVerse?
UniVerse is an academic social network designed to help university students communicate, collaborate, access campus resources and stay informed through verified department channels and student-led communities.
2. Who can use UniVerse?
UniVerse is intended for university students, faculty, and authorized campus participants. Some universities may restrict access to verified email domains or partner deployments.
3. Is UniVerse free to use?
Core UniVerse features are free for students and staff. Certain premium features or enterprise integrations may be offered under paid plans for universities or partners.
4. What platforms is UniVerse available on?
UniVerse is available on Android (Google Play and APK), web, and will be available on iOS via the App Store — TestFlight invites are offered for early access.
5. How do I get started?
Download the app or visit the web site, create an account using your email, verify the email address and complete profile setup (university, department, level) to receive tailored content.
6. How does UniVerse differ from generic social apps?
UniVerse is built specifically for campus needs with features like department matching, verified captains, course channels, timetables and resource distribution, plus privacy and moderation tuned for academic communities.
7. Can I use UniVerse offline?
Some content is cached for offline viewing, but core features such as messaging, sync and notifications require an internet connection.
8. How do I report abuse or violations?
Use the in-app reporting tools on a message or profile, or email help.universe.app@gmail.com with details and evidence. Our moderation team will review and take action as needed.
9. Where can I find feature updates?
Feature updates and release notes are published on our Changelog and Roadmap pages; subscribe to our announcements for major releases.
10. Who operates UniVerse?
UniVerse is operated by the UniVerse team; contact and company details are available on the About and Team pages. For partnerships or enterprise inquiries use the Contact form.

Accounts & Security

11. How do I verify my email?
After signing up, UniVerse sends a verification email with a secure link. Click the link to verify your address; if the link expires, request a new verification email from account settings.
12. I didn’t receive a verification email — what now?
Check your spam/junk folder, ensure the email address is correct, and request a new verification. If problems persist, contact support with your registered email address.
13. How do I reset my password?
Use the "Forgot password" link at sign-in to receive a secure password reset email. Follow the instructions to set a new password; password resets are handled through Firebase Auth flows.
14. Can I use a university single sign-on (SSO)?
Some universities may enable SSO integrations. If your university supports SSO with UniVerse, the option will appear during sign-up or account settings.
15. How do I change my email or recover a hacked account?
For email changes, follow the secure verify-before-change-email flow in account settings. For suspected compromise, contact support immediately and use account recovery processes described in Help Center.
16. What should I do if I lose access to my registered email?
Contact support and provide proof of account ownership (previous login locations, student ID, or other verification). We’ll guide you through a secure recovery process.
17. Does UniVerse support two-factor authentication (2FA)?
We support secure authentication practices; 2FA options are planned and may be available depending on the university policy or account tier. Check account security settings for available options.
18. How are passwords stored?
UniVerse uses Firebase Authentication which follows industry best practices for secure password storage and verification; passwords are never stored in plaintext on our servers.
19. How do I delete my account?
Use the Delete Account flow in Settings or visit the Delete Account page for step-by-step instructions. Deletion requests are processed promptly in accordance with our retention and legal obligations.
20. What happens to my content when I delete my account?
Public profile data is removed; private messages and files are deleted or anonymized where technically feasible. Shared content that other users retain may persist but personal identifiers will be anonymized where possible.

Features & Usage

21. How do I join my department group?
Complete your profile with university, faculty, department and level details, then use Department Matching in Discover or the onboarding wizard to join relevant groups and channels.
22. What are Department Captains and how do they help?
Department Captains are vetted student representatives who moderate department channels, approve notices and help surface official information; they reduce misinformation and centralize communication.
23. How do course discussion channels work?
Course channels are organized spaces within UniVerse where students and staff can post announcements, share notes, pin handouts and ask questions; moderators and captains can pin resources and manage access.
24. Can I upload files and handouts?
Yes — upload files, images and documents to channels or messages. Files are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage with access controls; large files may be subject to size and format guidelines.
25. How does the GPA & CGPA calculator work?
Enter your course units and grades into the GPA/CGPA tool to compute your academic averages and simulate outcomes. You can save or export results for personal use.
26. Can staff publish official notices?
Yes — staff and verified captains can publish notices and timetables which are displayed prominently to the affected groups; this ensures important communications are not missed.
27. Are there limits to message history?
Message retention policies vary by university and channel settings; UniVerse stores message history to support practical usage but also provides data export and deletion options in line with privacy policies.
28. How do notifications work?
UniVerse uses push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) for urgent messages, mentions and official notices; users can control notification settings per channel and in-app preferences.
29. Can I follow specific users or channels?
Yes — follow channels, courses and users to receive updates in your feed; followers and friends features help you stay connected with peers.
30. How do I report incorrect or outdated notices?
Use the in-app report option on the notice or contact the relevant captain or support with details so moderators can review and, if necessary, retract or update the notice.
31. Can I export my data or messages?
You can request a data export for account data and content; UniVerse provides machine-readable exports in line with privacy and legal requirements as described in the Privacy Policy.
32. Is there a way to bookmark or save posts?
Yes — use the save/bookmark action on posts and resources to keep them in your personal library for easy access later.
33. Can I restrict who messages me?
Privacy controls let you restrict direct messages to friends or followers only, and you can block or mute users at any time to prevent unwanted contact.
34. How do groups differ from channels?
Groups are community spaces often centred on students or clubs with membership controls; channels are topic-focused streams (e.g., course channels or announcement channels) within groups or departments.
35. What is a Verified Badge?
A Verified Badge identifies an account as authentic (staff, captains, or official organizations) after successful verification; it improves trust and may unlock moderation capabilities.

Captains, Verification & Community

36. How do I apply to be a Department Captain?
Apply via the Verify page and submit the required documentation (student ID, nomination, or staff endorsement); UniVerse and university partners review applications and activate captain badges on approval.
37. What are the responsibilities of a captain?
Captains moderate department channels, approve official notices, assist with onboarding, and liaise with staff and UniVerse for escalation and policy enforcement.
38. How are captains nominated?
Existing captains, staff, or recognized student organizations can nominate candidates; nominations include supporting evidence and are verified during the approval process.
39. Can captains be removed?
Yes — captains may have their badge revoked for policy violations, inactivity, or at the request of university partners; appeals are handled through support channels.
40. How do verification documents get handled?
Verification documents are stored securely and used only for identity confirmation. UniVerse does not publish verification documents and minimizes retention in line with privacy requirements.
41. Can I see a list of captains for my department?
Yes — department pages list active captains and verified accounts; contact details or office hours may be shown if provided by the captain.
42. Do captains have special moderation tools?
Captains have tools to pin notices, approve resources, and flag content for UniVerse moderation, but sensitive moderation actions are reviewed by the moderation team to avoid misuse.
43. Are captains paid or volunteer roles?
Most captain roles are volunteer positions supported by recognition and platform tools. Some universities may offer stipends or academic credit depending on local arrangements.
44. How does UniVerse prevent captain impersonation?
Verification badges, documentation checks and staff endorsements reduce risk of impersonation; impersonation reports trigger immediate review and possible suspension.
45. What if my department has no captain?
Students may nominate a captain or contact UniVerse partnerships to coordinate with the university for an official nomination and verification process.

Developers & Integration

46. What backend services does UniVerse use?
UniVerse leverages Google Firebase for core services: Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage and Cloud Messaging among other cloud services and integrations.
47. Is there an API for UniVerse?
UniVerse exposes limited APIs and integration points for university partners; contact partnerships for access and integration support. Public API plans may be announced on the Roadmap.
48. How can universities integrate timetables or LMS?
We support integrations via secure connectors and data feeds; universities should contact partnerships to arrange secure, privacy-compliant integrations with LMS or timetable systems.
49. How are security rules enforced for data?
UniVerse uses Firebase security rules and role-based access controls to restrict reads/writes based on university, department and user roles, and performs regular audits of rules and logs.
50. Where do I add my Firebase configuration?
For client integration, add Firebase configuration in a secure configuration file or environment during deployment; do not commit API keys to public repositories — use CI/CD secrets for production deployments.
51. Can I self-host UniVerse?
UniVerse is delivered as a hosted SaaS platform; enterprise or university deployments may be arranged with dedicated hosting and contractual terms through partnerships.
52. How do I report a security vulnerability?
Report security issues to help.universe.app@gmail.com with detailed steps to reproduce and any relevant logs. We respond promptly and follow responsible disclosure practices.
53. Does UniVerse offer webhooks or event subscriptions?
Webhook/event subscription support is available for enterprise partners; contact partnerships to discuss use cases and secure implementation details.
54. What data formats are used for exports?
Data exports are provided in machine-readable formats such as JSON and CSV to facilitate portability and compliance with data subject requests.
55. Can I contribute to the front-end design system?
The UniVerse design system is maintained by the product design team; contributions from partners can be coordinated through design reviews and contribution agreements.

Privacy, Data & Legal

56. Where can I read the privacy policy?
The full Privacy Policy is available on the Privacy Policy page which explains data collection, use, retention and your rights as a user.
57. How does UniVerse use cookies?
UniVerse uses cookies for authentication, preferences and analytics; the Cookie Policy explains each cookie type and how to manage them in your browser.
58. Can I request deletion of my data?
Yes — submit a deletion request via the Delete Account page or contact support; we will process requests in accordance with legal obligations and our retention policy.
59. Will my data be shared with third parties?
UniVerse shares data only with service providers (e.g., Firebase) who act on our behalf, and with universities as required for course and departmental functionality; we do not sell personal data.
60. How long is my data retained?
Retention periods vary by data type: active account data is retained while your account exists, logs and backups have limited retention periods, and certain records may be retained to comply with legal obligations.
61. Does UniVerse comply with international data protection laws?
UniVerse is designed to respect applicable data protection laws and international transfers; specific compliance (e.g., GDPR) depends on contractual arrangements with universities and data processing details.
62. How do I exercise data access or portability rights?
Contact help.universe.app@gmail.com with your request. We will verify your identity and provide data exports or take corrective actions in accordance with applicable law.
63. Are backups encrypted?
Backups are stored with encryption at rest and in transit, and access to backup data is restricted and audited by our operations team.
64. Who is the data controller for UniVerse?
UniVerse, as the operating organization, acts as the data controller for user data; specific university deployments may have different arrangements which will be documented in partnership agreements.

Troubleshooting & Support

65. The app crashes — what should I do?
Collect app logs, device info, OS version and steps to reproduce, then report the issue via the in-app Bug Report or email support; include screenshots if possible to help our engineers diagnose the problem quickly.
66. I can't receive push notifications — how do I fix it?
Ensure notifications are enabled in your device settings and in-app settings; confirm the app has permission and that network connectivity is available. Contact support if issues persist.
67. Messages are delayed — why?
Delays can be caused by network issues, server-side throttling or background restrictions on your device. Check connectivity and app background permissions and report systemic delays to support.
68. How do I clear the app cache?
On Android, clear the app cache via Settings > Apps > UniVerse > Storage > Clear Cache. On the web, clear site data or use an incognito window for a quick check.
69. I reported a user — what happens next?
Our moderation team reviews reports, may request additional evidence, and takes action ranging from warnings to suspensions. You will be notified of the outcome where appropriate.
70. How do I recover deleted content?
Deleted content recovery depends on retention and backups; contact support immediately with details — recovery may be possible within a limited timeframe in accordance with policies.
71. How can I speed up the app?
Keep the app updated, disable unnecessary background syncs, free device storage and use Wi-Fi for large uploads; contact support for device-specific optimization tips if needed.
72. The website shows a certificate or loading error — what should I do?
Refresh the page, check your system date/time, and try a different browser. If the error persists, report it to support with a screenshot and the URL for investigation.

Downloads, Deployment & Admin

73. Where can I download the Android app?
Download the app from Google Play via the Download page or use the direct APK link provided on the Download page for offline installation (verify SHA256 checksum before installing).
74. Why is the APK not installing?
Ensure "Install unknown apps" permission is enabled for the installing app, confirm the APK is compatible with your device and verify the APK signature and checksum before installing.
75. How do I deploy the website to Firebase Hosting?
Use the Firebase CLI: install firebase-tools, run firebase login, initialize hosting with firebase init hosting, and deploy with firebase deploy --only hosting. See README for details.
76. Can my university host a private instance?
Private or enterprise deployments are available via partnership agreements; contact our partnerships team to discuss dedicated hosting and compliance requirements.
77. How do I configure SEO and social previews?
Provide og-image, meta tags and structured data; the site includes templates for Open Graph and JSON-LD which can be customized for each page in production.
78. How do I integrate Firebase credentials securely?
Do not commit credentials to source control; inject config at build/deploy time using environment variables or CI secrets. Use server-side functions for sensitive operations.
79. How can admins monitor service health?
Use the Status page for quick checks and integrate UniVerse with your observability stack or Firebase Monitoring for enterprise-grade alerts and dashboards.
80. Who do I contact for partnership integrations?
Contact partnerships via the Contact page or email help.universe.app@gmail.com to start integration discussions and schedule a demo or pilot.
