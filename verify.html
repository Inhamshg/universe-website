Account verification

Verification helps users trust official accounts — for staff, department captains and university organizations.

What is verification?

Verification is a badge indicating that UniVerse has validated the identity of a user or organization. Verified accounts are given elevated trust and access to certain moderation or announcement features.

Who can apply?

  • University staff & offices
  • Official student organizations
  • Department Captains after nomination and vetting

How to apply

  1. Prepare documentation (staff email, letter from department, student ID or organizational registration).
  2. Visit the verification form (in-app or contact support via help.universe.app@gmail.com).
  3. Submit documents and a short statement of purpose for the verification badge.
  4. UniVerse reviews submissions and responds within 7-14 business days.

Privacy & security

Documentation submitted for verification is used only for identity confirmation and is stored securely. We do not share verification documents publicly.

Appeals

If your application is declined you may appeal by providing additional documentation and contacting support.
